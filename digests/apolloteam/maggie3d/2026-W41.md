### Maggie destination blending: TEXMODE add/mul only copy texel on beta core
zyber.se found TEXMODE add ($44) and mul ($84) just copy the texel on a beta core; thellier_32275 said older cores did ADD and MUL without an enable bit.

- thellier_32275 could not confirm recent cores, as the latest core tried had buggy textures.
- Blend functions worked were ONE,ONE and ZERO,SRCCOLOR, used for Quake lightmaps.

❓ **Open:** Whether recent cores support destination blending remains unconfirmed.
