### ADPCM planned for ARNE audio chip; Rudi shows biquad-filter audio on MiSTer
Arne is adding ADPCM to the audio DMA to cut loading times to about a quarter; rudi09421 has ADPCM decoding and a chained IIR biquad filter running in a MiSTer core.

- ADPCM files are about 1/4 the size of PCM
- rudi09421's pipeline supports channel chaining for equalizers and delayed channels for simple reverb
- rudi09421 also added ham03-ham08 modes to the MiSTer core
