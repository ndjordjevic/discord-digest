### Choosing 020/030/060 builds on Vampire V1200 V2: 080 and FPU builds matter most
biggun68080 advised mvk1993 that 020, 030, 040 and 060 builds perform almost alike on the Vampire, while FPU support and native 080 builds make the real difference.

- Rule of thumb from biggun68080: 020 is about 4% faster than 000; 030/040/060 differ by roughly 3% either way.
- 080 compile flag can be about 20% faster; AMMX use can be up to 400% faster for GFX, video, JPEG and MP3 code.
- Softfloat builds can run far slower than FPU builds, so pick the FPU variant for fractal or 3D programs.
