### New A6000 owners: USB ports, joypad mode and scanline command
makis9269's dead USB ports and joypad problems came from using the wrong port and wrong controller mode; dangassman was told how to disable scanlines.

- Joypad goes in the upper right-side port and must be in D-mode, not X-mode (red ring light).
- Disable scanlines with `ApolloControl SC=0` in the startup-sequence.
