### zyber.se's corrupted CF card on A6000 fixed by reformatting DH12:
With ApolloSFSDoctor unreleased, willemdrijver said the only option was reformatting DH12:; zyber.se formatted and restored a backup, and it works now.

- willemdrijver advised Quick format, never a full format on CF or SD cards.
- Backup content to an SD card first; the filesystem is SFS, so `pfsdoctor` does not apply.
