### Recurring CF-card blockid errors and whether bad blocks can be marked
zyber.se kept getting blockid errors on the Personal: partition; people suggested HDToolBox bad-block marking, but biggun68080 said CF cards remap blocks internally and a soft filesystem break is more likely.

- anti_cmos said HDToolBox on CoffinOS can set badblocks
- other_pisklak: marking might work on OFS/FFS, unclear on PFS or SFS

### Finding monitors that truly output 50Hz for A6000 and other Amigas
torsten_28547 reported Dell U2410 monitors run 60Hz only; others suggested AOC 24E1Q and 24G2U, while supremeturnip said their U2410 works at 50 and 60Hz.
