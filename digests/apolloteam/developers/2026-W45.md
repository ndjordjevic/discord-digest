### 📦 tjomp adds 68080 and AMMX support to Devpac's GenAm
tjomp released a GenAm with 68080 support that drops into the Devpac folder, and explained the bank prefix used for extended registers.

- The assembler emits a bank prefix so the CPU reads move.l d0,e0 as intended.
- Extended registers on AmigaOS need `ori.w #$0800,sr`.
- kamelito noted GenAm can also cross-assemble via Vamos.
