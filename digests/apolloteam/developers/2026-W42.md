### tjomp benchmarks 68080 copy loops: AMMX unrolling helps only outside cache
tjomp measured AMMX and MOVE16 copy loops on 68080; unrolling AMMX gave no gain in cache and a small gain out of cache.

- biggun68080 suggested MOVE16 and noted the CPU combines writes and prefetches reads
- move16 does not give a free dbf, and move.l does not fuse when reading from memory
- tjomp proposes unrolling 2 or 4 load/store pairs in loops, move16 for unlooped copies
