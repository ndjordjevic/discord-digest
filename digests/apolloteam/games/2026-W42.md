### Screamer port WIP on Vampire 1200 reaches 30-35FPS at 320x200
cego1200 showed Screamer running on a V1200 at 15, then 18FPS in 640x400, and 30-35FPS in 320x200; biggun68080 reviewed the optimization approach.

- Renderer uses direct pointer flip and TEX8 with 64-bit stores; texel cache misses are the next target
- Goal: 25FPS at 640x400, then a V4+Maggie version
- Not yet released
