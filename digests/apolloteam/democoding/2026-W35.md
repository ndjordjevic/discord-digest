### Advice for a SAGA parallax scroller in C on an A6000
.hellbeard asked whether vbcc is the way to code a simple parallax demo for SAGA in C, and received tool and approach suggestions from other_pisklak and biggun68080.

- other_pisklak said vbcc is nice but old gcc2 9.5 still produces the best quality code; pointed to ApolloGenie for crossdev and said ApollOS may come with a dev system already configured.
- biggun68080 said SAGA coding is relatively easy: set the GFX mode and the PTR to the frame, and a display already exists.
- biggun68080 warned C carries a performance disadvantage, that AMMX/ASM has a big advantage for GFX operations, and that AMMX is best for a demo that relies on copying a lot of data.
- .hellbeard's plan is something simple at 720p or lower with about 5 layers that need not be full screen, similar to the forest scene of their Evoke 2026 entry Glyphnosis; .hellbeard has migrated to OS 3.2 and could use inline assembly.
- .hellbeard asked for C code examples for SAGA and an IDE for gcc; no examples were given in the thread.

### FRED-80 demos on Apollo hardware cheer up a viewer; community explains FRED-80
rogue_particle thanked the author of an AI-written FRED-80 demo running on real Apollo V4SA hardware, and tjomp explained what FRED-80 is.
