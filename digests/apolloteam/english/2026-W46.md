### 📦 DockAwesome 1.0 beta2 released for ApolloOS, Coffin and AmigaOS 3.2.3
zyber.se released DockAwesome beta2 with wider OS compatibility, faster Folder Stacks and fixes; testers reported a grey rectangle on OS 3.2.3 and zyber.se is investigating.

- Folder Stack loading dropped from 0.9s to 0.13s; macOS-style zoom up to 128 px; live PiP transparency.
- michael_1260 and kamelito saw a grey rectangle (PiP layer) on OS 3.2.3, core 12856; zyber.se couldn't reproduce and says today's version should fix it.
- Bug reports should include the output of `DockAwesome --caps`.

❓ **Open:** Whether the grey rectangle is fixed in the latest build is awaiting kamelito's report.
