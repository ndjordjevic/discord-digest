### tjomp explains Apollo V4 SAGA audio to veneficvs for sampler and synth work
tjomp and biggun68080 explained V4 audio: 16 DMA channels, 16-bit stereo, no effects but volume and panning; veneficvs learned how to approach sample export and softsynths.

- Export samples as 16-bit 44kHz stereo big-endian RAW; tjomp converts WAV with ffmpeg.
- Channels are like synth polyphony, not DAW tracks; software mixing gives more voices.
- MIDI expansion is planned by Apollo Team without release date; the V4SA has SPI ports.
